SATURN

  • Position: Sixth planet from the Sun.
  • Size: Second-largest planet in the solar system, about nine times wider than Earth.
  • Composition: A gas giant made mostly of hydrogen and helium, meaning it has no solid surface.

MERCURY

  • Orbit Speed: It zips around the Sun at about 29 miles per second (47 km/s), making it the fastest planet in the solar system.
  • Day Length: A single rotation on its axis takes 59 Earth days, but a full solar day (sunrise to sunrise) takes 176 Earth days

VENUS

  • Hottest Planet: Surface temperatures reach about 872°F (467°C), which is hot enough to melt lead. This happens because its thick atmosphere traps heat in a runaway greenhouse effect

URANUS
